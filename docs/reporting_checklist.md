# Proposed reporting checklist

For studies of acute kidney injury using routinely collected data.

1. The total eligible cohort, before any creatinine availability requirement.
2. The distribution of creatinine results per patient, and the proportion with none.
3. Where recorded, the timing of draws, including the share drawn on scheduled rounds.
4. The baseline rule as applied, including whether values within 7 days of the index episode were admitted.
5. The proportion of baselines from each tier: 7–365 days before, within 7 days before, or after the index episode.
6. The number and proportion evaluable at each level, as a percentage of the total cohort.
7. The denominator used for every reported incidence, stated beside the estimate.
8. The proportion of non-evaluability attributable to death rather than to non-measurement.
9. Where a hierarchy baseline was unavailable for a substantial minority, incidence under an alternative baseline as a sensitivity analysis.
